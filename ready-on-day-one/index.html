How it was made

This is a sandbox project. I use pieces like this to test new tools for content, design, and development for my classes and for program marketing.

Script & build

  • Claude for scripting, storyboarding, and orchestrating the build
  • HyperFrames: code-driven (HTML/CSS/GSAP) motion graphics and kinetic typography

Generative media

  • Higgsfield AI for the synthetic voiceover
  • Seedance 2.5 (via Higgsfield) for text-to-video B-roll

Sound & finish

  • Python (NumPy/SciPy) for the synthesized music bed and sound effects
  • FFmpeg for mixing, muxing, and compression

The process

  1. Define the story, audience, and proof points.
  2. Script scene by scene and generate the voiceover.
  3. Build each scene as animated HTML, timed to the narration.
  4. Layer generative B-roll behind the graphics.
  5. Compose music and sound effects, ducked under the voice.
  6. Render, review, iterate with a human in the loop.
In short: programmatic, AI-assisted motion design. A rapid-prototyping pipeline from idea to polished, brand-consistent video in hours, and easy to version because every scene is code.
