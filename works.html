Tahoe Times

A 33-second hand-drawn pencil animation, made entirely with AI.

A couple road-trips around Lake Tahoe in a VW Westfalia, pulls over for photos, hops a guardrail to reach the shore, and shares a kiss at sunset. The look is graphite pencil on off-white paper, cross-hatched and animated on twos with a subtle "boiling line" wobble. Black and gray only, with a 70s folk-rock mood.

Tech stack: Nano Banana Pro for the first-frame sketch and character reference; Seedance 2.5 on Higgsfield for video (three 10-second image-to-video clips, using reference images to keep faces and style consistent, each starting from the last frame of the one before); Epidemic Sound for the music (two tracks).
